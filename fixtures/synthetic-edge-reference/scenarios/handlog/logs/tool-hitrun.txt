[2mEach night ends in one exit, judged on the LAST time they left (a break they came back from is not).
Stay = dealt in during the night's last 10 hands.  Play = share of each night's hands dealt to them.
Up / Down = left for good with chips, 5+ big blinds ahead / behind.
(About even, and taken out by the admin: in the player view.)
Bust = went under 1 big blind and went home.  Rebuy = busted and bought back in.
Back = gone 30+ hands, then dealt in again.
When up = share of the night (from their sit-down) still to come when they left, on nights they
finished ahead, weighted by the win (50+ big blinds counts fully).  Staying counts as 0%.
When dn = the same on nights they finished behind with chips.
Hit = the part of When up that came right after their best point of the night, with most of it
kept: full credit leaving at the peak, half 20 hands later, a sixteenth 80 hands later.
All three lean toward the pool's average for a player with few such nights.
H&R = 100 x (When up + how much When up exceeds When dn + Hit) / 3.
An H&R in (brackets) has fewer than 3 winning nights; the only tag it can get is Busts out.
